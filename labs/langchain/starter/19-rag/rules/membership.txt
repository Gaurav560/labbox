Membership

Any farmer with land in Pimpalgaon or the six neighbouring villages can join. The one-time share is Rs 1,000 and the
yearly fee Rs 200. A new member brings an Aadhaar card, the 7/12 land record and two photographs; the member number is
given the same day.

Members vote at the annual meeting in August and receive a share of the co-op's profit in proportion to the produce
they sold through it.
