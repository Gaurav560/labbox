Cold storage

The co-op's cold store at Pimpalgaon keeps onions, tomatoes and grapes. The charge is Rs 2 per crate per day, paid
when the crates are taken out. Bookings of up to 50 crates are confirmed at the counter. Bookings of more than 50
crates need the manager's approval, which usually takes one working day.

Crates must be clean and labelled with the member's number. Onions are stored at 2 degrees, tomatoes at 10 degrees and
grapes at 0 degrees, each in its own chamber. Crates left more than 120 days are moved to the overflow store and
charged double.
