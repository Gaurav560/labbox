Spraying and harvest

Members must record every spray in the co-op's spray diary: the date, the product and the field. Grapes may not be
harvested for 10 days after any fungicide spray, and crates from a field without a complete spray diary are not
accepted for export.

The co-op does not advise on products or doses. For a disease or pest problem, show a sample to the co-op's agronomist,
Mrs. Kulkarni, who visits farms on Tuesdays and Fridays and is at the office on Saturday mornings.
