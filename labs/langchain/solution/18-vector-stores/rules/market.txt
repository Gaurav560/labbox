Selling through the co-op

The co-op sells members' onions, tomatoes and grapes at the Pimpalgaon mandi and to exporters. The day's mandi rates
are posted at the office by 10:00 and given by Navin. The co-op's commission is 2% of the sale price.

Payment reaches the member's bank account within 3 working days of the sale. Export grapes must meet the exporter's
quality grade; crates that fail the grade are sold at the mandi instead.
