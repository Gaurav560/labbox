Crop loans

Members can borrow up to Rs 50,000 per acre for seeds, fertiliser and labour, at 7% a year. A loan is repaid in one
instalment after harvest, at the latest 9 months after it was taken.

To apply, bring the 7/12 land record, the member's passbook and an estimate of the season's costs to the office. Loans
above Rs 2,00,000 are approved by the committee, which meets on the first Monday of each month. A member with an unpaid
loan from an earlier season cannot take a new one.
