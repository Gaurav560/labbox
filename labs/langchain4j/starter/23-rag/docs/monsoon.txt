Monsoon travel

From June to September, heavy rain can make the ghat section unsafe. When rainfall at Lonavala passes 100 mm in an
hour, trains stop at the nearest station until the track is inspected, usually for 30 to 90 minutes. Delays are
announced on platforms and sent by SMS to the phone number on the booking. If the line is closed for more than four
hours, the train is cancelled and every passenger gets a full refund.
