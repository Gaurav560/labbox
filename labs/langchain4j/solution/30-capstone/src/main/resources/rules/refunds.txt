Refunds and cancellations

A ticket cancelled 24 hours or more before departure is refunded in full. A ticket cancelled less than 24 hours before
departure is refunded at half the fare. No refund is given after the train has left. Refunds are paid to the card or
account used for booking within 5 working days.

When Sahyadri Rail cancels a train, for example because of a landslide on the ghat, every passenger gets a full refund,
whenever they cancel, or may travel on the next train with a free seat on the same ticket.
