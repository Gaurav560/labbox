# The planetarium dome

Gallery: Space Gallery (Gallery 1)

The planetarium is a 15 metre dome with 120 reclining seats. A star projector in the middle shows about 9,000 stars,
the planets and the Milky Way, as they look from a dark place far from city lights.

Shows run at 11:00 (Journey to the Moon), 14:00 (The Life of Stars) and 16:00 (Black Holes), from Tuesday to Sunday.
Each show lasts 35 minutes. Planetarium tickets are sold separately from museum tickets, and weekend shows often sell
out, so booking ahead is a good idea.
