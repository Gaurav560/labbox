# The solar tree

Gallery: Energy Gallery (Gallery 5)

A steel tree whose leaves are solar panels. On a sunny day it makes enough electricity for all the lights in the Energy
Gallery. Solar panels turn light directly into electricity, with no moving parts.

India has some of the largest solar parks in the world, such as Bhadla in Rajasthan, and solar power has become one of
the cheapest ways to make electricity. A screen beside the tree shows how much it is making right now.
