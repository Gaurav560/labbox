# The bicycle generator

Gallery: Energy Gallery (Gallery 5)

Pedal an exercise bicycle and try to light a row of ten bulbs. The bicycle turns a generator: a coil of wire spinning
between magnets, which makes electric current, as Michael Faraday discovered in 1831.

Old-style bulbs need about 60 watts each; LED bulbs give the same light with about 8. Most visitors can light all ten
LEDs, but only two or three old bulbs. Every power station, from coal to wind, uses a generator like this, only bigger.
