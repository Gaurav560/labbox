# The prism room

Gallery: Light Gallery (Gallery 4)

A beam of white sunlight enters through the roof and passes through a large glass prism, spreading into a rainbow across
the floor. White light is a mix of all colours, and glass bends each colour by a slightly different amount: violet the
most, red the least. Isaac Newton showed this with prisms in 1666.

Rainbows in the sky work the same way, with raindrops instead of a prism. Stand with your back to the low sun after rain
in the monsoon to see one.
