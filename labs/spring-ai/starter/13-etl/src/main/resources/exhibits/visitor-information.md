# Visitor information

## Opening hours

The museum is open from Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 18:00. Last entry is at 17:00. The museum is closed on Mondays.

## Tickets and prices

A museum ticket costs 120 rupees for adults, and 60 rupees for children aged 5 to 15 and for students. Children under 5
enter free. Planetarium shows cost 80 rupees extra per person. School groups that book ahead visit free on Wednesdays.

## Getting here

The museum is on Ganeshkhind Road in Pune. The nearest metro station is a ten minute walk away. Lockers and wheelchairs
are free at the entrance, and every gallery has a lift.
