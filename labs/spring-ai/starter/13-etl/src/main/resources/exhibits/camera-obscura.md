# The camera obscura

Gallery: Light Gallery (Gallery 4)

Step into a dark room with a tiny hole in one wall, and an upside-down picture of the street outside appears on the
opposite wall, in colour, moving. This is a camera obscura ("dark room" in Latin), the ancestor of every camera.

Light travels in straight lines, so rays from the top of a tree pass through the hole and land low on the wall, and rays
from the bottom land high. The Arab scientist Ibn al-Haytham described it about a thousand years ago.
