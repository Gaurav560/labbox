# Rajasaurus, a dinosaur from India

Gallery: Life Gallery (Gallery 3)

A cast of the skull of Rajasaurus narmadensis, "the royal lizard of the Narmada". Its bones were found in the 1980s near
Rahioli in Gujarat, and the species was named in 2003. It lived about 67 million years ago, at the very end of the age
of dinosaurs.

Rajasaurus was a meat-eater about nine metres long, with a short horn on its head, probably for display. Its relatives
lived in Madagascar and South America, which were joined to India in a supercontinent long before.
