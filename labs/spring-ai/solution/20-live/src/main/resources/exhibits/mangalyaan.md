# Mangalyaan, India's Mars Orbiter Mission

Gallery: Space Gallery (Gallery 1)

A one-to-five scale model of Mangalyaan, the Mars Orbiter Mission of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). It
was launched on 5 November 2013 and entered orbit around Mars on 24 September 2014, making India the first country to
reach Mars orbit on its first attempt, and the first in Asia.

The mission cost about 450 crore rupees, less than many films about space. Built to last six months, it kept working
for about eight years, sending back photographs of Mars and studying its thin atmosphere.
