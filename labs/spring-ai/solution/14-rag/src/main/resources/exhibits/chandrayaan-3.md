# Chandrayaan-3 and the Vikram lander

Gallery: Space Gallery (Gallery 1)

A full-size model of the Vikram lander of Chandrayaan-3. On 23 August 2023 Vikram landed near the Moon's south pole,
the first spacecraft ever to land in that region. India became the fourth country to land softly on the Moon.

Vikram carried a small rover, Pragyan, which drove about 100 metres and confirmed sulphur in the lunar soil. The landing
day, 23 August, is now celebrated in India as National Space Day.
