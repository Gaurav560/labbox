# The DNA double helix

Gallery: Life Gallery (Gallery 3)

A two-storey model of DNA, the molecule that carries the instructions for every living thing. Its shape, a twisted ladder
called a double helix, was worked out in 1953 by James Watson and Francis Crick, using X-ray photographs taken by
Rosalind Franklin.

Each rung of the ladder is a pair of bases: A with T, and C with G. Your DNA has about three billion such pairs. Press the
button at the foot of the model to light up the pairs one by one.
