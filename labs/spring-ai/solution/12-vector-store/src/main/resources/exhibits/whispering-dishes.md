# The whispering dishes

Gallery: Motion Gallery (Gallery 2)

Two large curved dishes face each other from opposite ends of the gallery, 15 metres apart. Whisper into the ring in
front of one dish, and a friend at the other dish hears you clearly.

Each dish is a parabola: it gathers sound waves that arrive straight on and reflects them all to one point, the ring.
The same shape gathers radio waves in a satellite dish and starlight in a telescope mirror.
