# Deccan Shield motor policy wording (two-wheelers and private cars), short form

C1 | Own damage | We pay for loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by accident, fire, explosion, lightning, flood, storm, hailstorm, earthquake or landslide.
C2 | Theft | We pay for theft of the insured vehicle. A police complaint (FIR) must be filed within 48 hours of discovering the theft, and a copy sent with the claim.
C3 | Deductible | Every own damage claim bears a compulsory deductible: Rs 1,000 for two-wheelers and Rs 2,000 for private cars. We pay the assessed loss minus the deductible.
C4 | Glass | Breakage of windscreen or windows is covered under own damage. Glass-only claims are paid without the deductible.
C5 | Reporting a claim | A claim must be reported within 7 days of the incident. Late reporting may be accepted if the delay is explained.
C6 | Photos and documents | Every own damage claim needs photos of the damage from four sides, the odometer, and the chassis number, together with the RC book and the driver's licence.
C7 | Engine damage after water | Damage to the engine caused by trying to start a vehicle standing in water is not covered, unless the engine protection add-on was bought.
C8 | Drunk driving | We do not pay for loss or damage while the driver was under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
C9 | Valid licence | We do not pay for loss or damage while the vehicle was driven by a person without a valid driving licence.
C10 | Total loss | When repair would cost more than 75% of the insured declared value (IDV), the vehicle is treated as a total loss and we pay the IDV minus the deductible.
C11 | Policy period | Cover applies only to incidents during the policy period shown in the schedule. Incidents after the policy has ended are not covered.
C12 | Wear and tear | We do not pay for wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, or tyres damaged without other damage to the vehicle.
C13 | Vandalism | Malicious damage by third parties, such as scratches or broken windows in parking, is covered under own damage, after the deductible.
C14 | Cashless repair | At network garages repairs can be cashless: we pay the garage directly, and the customer pays only the deductible and items not covered.
